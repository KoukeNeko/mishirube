# Archive

Screens taken out of the app because what they show does not exist yet.
They are kept, at the path they had under the project root, for when
the feature they sketch is built for real. Nothing here is compiled,
analysed or tested; imports still point at their old neighbours and
will need updating on the way back.

| File | Sketches | Why it left |
|---|---|---|
| `lib/features/me/sync_screen.dart` | Sync status, offline queue, a conflict to resolve | There is no sync. Every number on it was invented. |
| `lib/features/me/ai_permissions_screen.dart` | Bring-your-own-key provider and AI read/write scopes | There is no AI provider. It claimed a key was in the keychain. |
| `lib/features/nutrition/meal_entry_screen.dart` | Logging by photo or by voice | Both led to the same demo sandwich. |
| `lib/features/nutrition/meal_confirm_screen.dart` | Confirming what a photo was recognised as | It always recognised the demo sandwich. |

`ai_proposal_screen.dart` stays in the app: the insight detail page
still opens it.
